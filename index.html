Questions before you book?

Quick answers to the things customers usually want to know.

How long will the carpet take to dry?
Drying time depends on fibre, ventilation and how heavily the carpet is cleaned. This service currently quotes an average of 4–6 hours for typical jobs.
Can you remove pet stains and odours?
Yes. Pet stain treatment can be added to the booking. Results vary by the age and severity of the stain, so the technician confirms what is realistically achievable after inspection.
Do I need to move my furniture?
The site currently says furniture can be moved and replaced on protective tabs for standard carpet cleaning, subject to safe access and the item involved.
Is the online price guaranteed?
No. The prices shown are starting prices for standard soiling. The final price is confirmed once the space has been seen.
